## Design Context

### Users

Too Long is for people who receive long personal or work voice notes and want to understand the useful parts without leaving the macOS menu bar. Their primary job is to choose or drop a recording, get a trustworthy local transcript, and optionally create a concise recap without losing control of what leaves the Mac.

### Brand Personality

Modern, calm, and lightly playful. The product should feel candid and capable rather than corporate, technical, or overtly “AI.” Privacy should feel like a normal product quality, not a warning.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a refined native-macOS direction built around the logo’s indigo and aqua palette. Neutral surfaces are softly tinted toward indigo, with restrained Liquid Glass used to clarify hierarchy rather than decorate every element. Support both light and dark appearances. Avoid earthy colors, neon-on-black AI styling, heavy gradients, excessive glow, and nested cards.

Typography should feel sober and capable. Use the standard macOS sans-serif design with regular, medium, and semibold weights; avoid rounded or novelty faces and reserve bold emphasis for genuinely important states.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next action unmistakable: choosing, dropping, stopping, copying, and starting another note should always be easy to find.
2. Keep context visible: opening a file picker or starting a long-running task must not make the app appear to disappear.
3. Use indigo for primary actions and selection, aqua for progress and positive supporting accents, and semantic red only for errors.
4. Prefer progressive disclosure: transcription works without setup; provider credentials and model details stay in Settings.
5. Communicate privacy at the decision point with short, plain language.
6. Preserve native macOS behavior, keyboard access, contrast, reduced-motion preferences, and clear focus states.
